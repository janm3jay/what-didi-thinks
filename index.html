How it works

1

Export your chat

Export any WhatsApp chat, one-on-one or group, without media.

Android
  1. Open the chat and tap ⋮
  2. More → Export chat
  3. Choose Without media
  4. Save the .txt file
iPhone
  1. Open the chat and tap the name at the top
  2. Export Chat → Without Media
  3. Save to Files (you'll get a .zip)
2

Upload it here

Drop in the .txt or .zip. It's read in your browser, and the numbers are worked out on your phone, not on a server.

Questions

Is my chat private?

Your file is opened in your browser. The stats are worked out on your device. A text sample is sent to the AI to write the report, and it isn't stored or used for anything else.

Which chats work?

WhatsApp exports from Android (.txt) and iPhone (.zip), in any language and date format. Works for one-on-one chats and groups.

Will Didi take my side?

No. Didi is fair to everyone in the chat, including the people who aren't you. That's the point.

What about really long chats?

Every message is counted for the stats. For the written report, Didi reads the start, the most recent stretch, and slices from the middle, so she can see how things changed.
